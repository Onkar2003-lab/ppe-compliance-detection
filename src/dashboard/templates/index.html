3 · Rules

Someone must be in the zone without their PPE for this long before it counts as an incident. 0 alerts the instant they are seen; a few seconds ignores people walking past. Alarm on-delay — ANSI/ISA-18.2, EEMUA 191.

A helmet the camera loses for a moment is still on the worker's head, so it stays believed for this long. Set it to 0 and people flicker between pass and fail. Hysteresis on the per-person PPE state.

Lower finds more people and more false boxes; higher is stricter and misses more. Confidence threshold — 0.25 is the value the reported results were scored at, so the demo behaves like the numbers.
